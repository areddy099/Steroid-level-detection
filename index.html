Reference information

  • Why test? Natural milk contains trace steroids; illegal growth promoters or therapeutic corticosteroids raise levels above the natural range.
  • Screening: ELISA or rapid immunoassay strips for high-throughput first checks.
  • Confirmation: LC-MS/MS provides specific, quantitative confirmation of suspect results.
  • Sampling: collect from the bulk tank after mixing, chill to 4 °C, freeze if not tested within 24 h; record chain of custody.
  • Status rules: Safe < 70 % of limit · Watch 70–100 % · Violation > 100 %. Violations trigger a re-test and batch hold.
  • Good practice: run blanks and spiked controls each batch, track recovery, and calibrate against certified standards.
  • Limits: thresholds in this demo are illustrative. Replace them with limits from your regulator (e.g. FSSAI, Codex, EU 37/2010).
